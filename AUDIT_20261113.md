**The role of 6741.** It is the funder's own account: same key, first to trade, and the one that received the
`pinkuniforms` withdrawal. The complete tape adds something the earlier work did not have. Between 20:00 and 22:42:57 UTC
the Machado market saw 15 taker trades worth 1,082 USD, the last at about 0.031. Then 6741 bought:

- 22:42:57, 19,120 shares taken at an average of 0.048;
- 22:46:49, 15,000 shares taken at 0.10;
- from 22:54:19, a resting order at 0.30 that filled 47,420 shares by 23:00:15.

In the fifteen minutes from its first trade, 6741 supplied 14,304.71 of the 14,704.94 USD bought (97.3 %), against four
buyers in all. The move from 0.03 to 0.30 is its own
([tape](research_notes/early-detection-2026-11-13/data/machado_tape_20251009T2000_20251010T0900.csv)).

## 2. Errors and corrections

**E1. Purchases of the eight accounts are understated.** `investigations/followup-20261113/sources/data/nobel_accounts.csv`,
column `nobel_buy_cost_before_0900_utc`. The data API returns taker rows only unless `taker_only=false` is asked. The
column therefore misses every purchase made through a resting order.

| Account | Stated (taker rows) | Complete tape, 9 Oct 00:00 to 10 Oct 09:00 UTC |
|---|---:|---:|
| 6741 | 38,787.06 | 43,338.58 |
| fmaduro | 2,410.37 | 11,390.86 |
| trumpdeservesit | 10,286.32 | 10,286.32 |
| striketheboats | 17,046.95 | 17,878.31 |
| aenews0 | 8,618.80 | 10,816.28 |
| madurowilllose | 30,449.19 | 30,470.97 |
| pinkuniforms | 19,216.72 | 20,319.32 |
| dirtycup | 25,496.43 | 68,373.81 |
| **Total** | **152,311.84** | **212,874.45** |

The eight accounts made 21.9 % of all purchases of Machado Yes in that window (972,264 USD). Three of them redeemed more
shares than they held at 09:00:00 UTC, so they also bought after that time; those later purchases were not examined.

**E2. F-006 of `followup-20261113`.** "Seven of the eight accounts made their first Machado purchase after the public
repricing from 0.037 at 22:30 to 0.390 at 23:00 UTC." The times are right. The word "public" misleads: no trade and no
price move precedes 6741, and 6741 made the move (section 1). The seven others followed a price set by the first
account of their own funder. The finding's limit already says the purchases of 6741 "may be part of the cause"; the
tape shows they are the cause. Proposed wording: "after the first account of the same funder had lifted the price from
0.03 to 0.30".
