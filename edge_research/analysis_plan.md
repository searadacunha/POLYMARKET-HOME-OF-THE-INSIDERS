## Inputs and clocks
Fixed prefix of phi-live/market.jsonl, SHA256 and byte length in data/local_manifest.json; receive-time window 2026-11-14 00:00 to 2026-11-16 13:00 UTC. Last valid quote per asset per received second is retained with its actual received timestamp. Full book frames retained independently. Invalid/crossed/missing quotes and backwards receive times counted. Never reconstruct depth across deltas: deployed book_status contains desynchronization. Full frames prove only displayed depth at their timestamp. Unknown cancellations, message loss and clock skew prevent certified fills.

## Signals fixed before returns
Every integer UTC minute, latest quote received at or before t, age <=5s; price midpoint in [0.02,0.98]. Momentum uses midpoint at t-300s, also age <=5s.
D1 momentum: increase >=0.005; D2 reversal: decrease <=-0.005.
D3 imbalance: latest FULL frame age <=5s, top-three-level bid size/(bid+ask size)>=0.8. No delta-derived depth.
Control: same minute schedule, abs(momentum)<0.001. Compare mean net per share with controls on the same token, UTC hour and decile of midpoint; require >=5 available control outcomes. Matched difference descriptive: overlapping observations and selection on available exits, no causal claim.
B1 copy-large: public BUY >=1000 dollars, first observed in trades.jsonl; decision at received time, not chain trade time. Only YES tokens, same quote validity. No future leaderboard selection. Deduplicate by transaction, token, side, size, price; this cannot distinguish identical fills, report limitation. This is size-based copying, not trained wallet skill.
A: use existing ChangeNOW findings; count local funding-universe overlap. No matched PnL test without pre-decision labels, complete non-CN histories and capital. Do not substitute withdrawals for profits.
C: review verified funding paths and frozen cluster results; shared service != shared owner. No new test without a temporal ownership/funding table and sufficient events.
E: paired YES+NO sum of best asks at each minute, both quotes age <=2s, quote-synchronization difference <=2s, list gross violations below 1. Do not assume basket atomicity, depth or net profit.

## Execution diagnostic, not fill claim
Horizons 60,300,3600,21600,86400 seconds from actual entry. Latencies 1,5,30 seconds. First observed quote at or after decision+latency, maximum extra wait 5s; first quote at or after entry+horizon, maximum extra wait 5s. Entry at ask, exit at bid. Missing entry/exit is excluded AND counted; split crossing forbidden. No interpolation. Quote-to-quote outcomes are conditional on recorded quotes and optimistic execution diagnostics.
Cost scenarios per share: optimistic feeRate=0 and slippage=0; politics feeRate=0.04 and adverse slippage=0.001 each leg; stress feeRate=0.07 and slippage=0.002 each leg. Formula feeRate*p*(1-p) on BOTH entry and exit irrespective of profit, documented at https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees (read 2026-11-16). Historical exact per-token rates unavailable: scenarios, never verified charges. Slippage added as cash cost, no rebates. Position 10 shares for accounting only. Depth support separately: latest full book age<=2s at entry and exit; summed displayed quantity at the quoted best price must support 10 shares. Missing depth never replaced by infinity. Full book prices must match the execution BBO. Maximum displayed size = min(entry ask quantity, exit bid quantity), NOT a guaranteed capacity.
