## Case joining rules

1. Canonical keys: `chain_id`, exchange contract, `condition_id`, `asset_id`, Gamma event/market ID, `tx_hash` + log index, and exact interval in UTC. A topic label such as “Iran” is **not** a join key.
2. Store **publication time**, **market creation time**, **trader's order-fill time**, **first public news time**, **oracle proposal/dispute/finality time**, and **prosecutorial filing/status date** separately. A later article cannot move the event timestamp backwards.
3. Do not add up reported profits from two papers until wallet overlap, gross/net rules, open inventory and time windows are reconciled.
4. “Known insider” must be replaced by precise and time-dependent legal language: `alleged in regulator complaint`, `charged`, `convicted`, `pleaded`, `acquitted`, or `no legal label`; verify latest court state before publication.
5. For wallet funding links distinguish same public source, direct sender-recipient transaction, exchange deposit address, matched bridge/order, and operator identity (often **unknown**).
6. A source can cite another source; collapse derivative press copies into **one evidence lineage** rather than counting mentions as independent confirmations.
