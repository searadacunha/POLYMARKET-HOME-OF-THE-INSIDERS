# What the public record still cannot establish

| Question | Evidence already available | Missing observation and falsifiable test |
|---|---|---|
| Does a financial alert deserve analyst time? | The engine seals a first trigger, evidence and rule class. This run produced no alerts. The Nobel replay returns a known wallet amid a much larger queue. | Blindly review alerts and a probability sample of non-alert market-hours from the same future universe. Compare useful-lead yield per 100 analyst reviews with a large-trade baseline. |
| Was a trade an early response to public information? | The new review command binds archived response bytes, an event mapping and several clocks. A reviewed event source can refer to more than one outcome. | Record publication, first receipt, review and trigger independently for civil awards and scheduled releases. Test whether a putative lead survives the earliest corroborated outcome-specific public item. |
| Does a shared intermediary discriminate between traders? | Three Arbitrum pass-throughs have exact receipts, token identity and reconciled balances. Exchange, bridge and treasury services naturally route unrelated customers together. | Match controls on chain, token, week, amount, provider and intermediary activity. Compare the frequency and delay of balanced pass-throughs. No controller labels exist for the screened addresses. |
| Can historical exposure shifts add information? | Earlier matched excess was +2.42 points with interval [−1.03, +5.83]. Only eight TEST units had an eligible financial pair. The power gate passed four of five strong-injection seeds. | Rebuild a distributable tape and define a minimum positive-control power before TEST. Hold out both future events and repeatedly active wallet clusters. [Original falsification](../../investigations/collective-exposure-falsification/sources/FALSIFICATION_REPORT.md) |
| Can an observed price be traded? | Bounded history prevents future substitution. A chart has no size. The existing book study reports spread and incomplete reachable depth. | Collect both sides, quantities, sequence gaps, delays and contemporaneous fees. Reuse the cost gate. Simulate at a fixed small size, show unfilled orders and rerun at larger sizes. [Existing study](../../edge_research/) |
| Does the favorite result survive a true sporting clock? | 97 of 98 September MLB moneylines were created after T−7d. | Anchor to an independently timestamped league fixture, first listing and executable ask. Report the eligible fraction before scoring. Repeat across leagues and schedule revisions. |
| How complete is cross-chain identity? | Public case files cover Polygon, Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum and some TRON contacts. Provider labels and shared destinations frequently have several explanations. | Rebuild selected deposits across two independent data providers. Separate wallet, proxy, payer, route operator and ultimate beneficiary. Track service and unknown edges without transitive ownership inference. |
| Would activity, reversal or related-market screens improve triage? | The archive includes related markets, thin-liquidity screens and cohort-conditioned prices. A direct pair seen retrospectively can be discovered too late. | Freeze market family membership as originally observed. Compare signed exposure changes to a size-, price- and event-time-matched group. Include zero-alert event families and censored opportunities. |

A full universe, market-wide time-prior news corpus, synchronized order books and independent adjudicators are currently missing. Earlier results can be checked to different depths. Five of 16 additional archival collective-study tests depend on a private 1,276,301,312-byte SQLite tape. This limits independent raw-tape verification even when aggregates and hashes agree. The narrower checks required by the repository are tracked separately. For clarity on evidence levels, the six successful receipts and bracketing balances are **CONFIRMED**. Specific cases as historically useful queue entries are **SUPPORTED**. A coordinated controller or superior actionable lead yield is a **HYPOTHESIS**. Equating end-minus-seven-days with game-start-minus-seven-days is **REFUTED** for both measured MLB cohorts. Private knowledge, general live precision and the external favorite effect remain **UNRESOLVED**.
