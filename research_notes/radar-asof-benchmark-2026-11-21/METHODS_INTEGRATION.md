# What entered the research and the monitor

The integration uses original implementation. External source and data files were inspected at the revisions and SHA-256 values in the inventory. The public deliverable includes computed aggregates, methods, pointers and normalized primary-source observations. Third-party data tables and programs stay with their publishers. [Source manifest](sources.json) · [Independent arithmetic](external_replay.py)

| Source and rights observed | Decision | Measurable consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 0xinsider, CC BY 4.0 public research | Adopt explicit horizon clocks. Their backward-looking reference mark estimates an available price. Our forward-looking evaluation records the first subsequent observation within ten minutes and records its delay. | 228 late marks withheld on the quote archive. [Method](https://github.com/orcalayer/polymarket-insider-score-backtest) · [Results](results.json) |

Primary inputs for the three intermediary checks are the existing Relay order registry, Arbitrum JSON-RPC and Circle's published token contract. The two-source transaction match, token amount in atomic units, transfer logs for the complete intermediate window and balances immediately outside the window are independently testable. Two successful bridge orders alone leave open the possibility of unrelated receipts at the same address.
