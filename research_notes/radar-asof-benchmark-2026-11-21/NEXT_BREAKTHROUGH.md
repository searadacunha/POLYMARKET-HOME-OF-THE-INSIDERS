# Three studies with the largest expected research value

The following designs are proposals for future registration. Only the historical protocols and short observations linked in this change have been executed. Each design includes a stopping rule so an empty or negative sample is publishable.

| Priority | Study | Scientific value | Feasibility | Distinguishing result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blinded prospective alert review | High | Medium, requires independent reviewers | A comparable numerator and denominator for investigation usefulness |
| 2 | Public-information clocks on civilian scheduled events | High | Medium, requires durable source capture | Measures how many apparent leads survive realistic public-information timing |
| 3 | Base rates of financially reconciled shared routes | High | Medium to high on public EVM chains | Converts a demonstrable payment pattern into a testable discriminator |

## 1. Blinded prospective alert review

Freeze the covered civilian market families, two detector versions, minimum eligible liquidity at decision time, block lag and daily review capacity. Register the three comparisons and the analysis code before collection. Feed both detectors the same timestamped stream. Seal every alert, gap and uptime interval. Sample non-alert market-hours with recorded inclusion probabilities across the same time and liquidity strata. Two independent reviewers receive identical delayed evidence packets with detector identity and market outcome hidden. Label a useful lead only when a specified, independently verifiable fact justifies additional investigation. Resolve disagreement through a third review and retain all votes.

Primary endpoint is useful leads per 100 reviews against a size-only baseline. Report coverage and analyst minutes, design-weighted precision and recall for that review definition, false-positive burden, inter-rater agreement and event-clustered uncertainty. An initial 200 independent alerted event groups gives a worst-case binomial 95% half-width of about seven points before cluster and sampling adjustments. Stop at the registered sample target or 90 days, report realized precision, and withhold a superiority claim if the corrected lower bound does not exceed zero. The current engine run supplies no estimate for that target. [Current performance](PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARK.md)

## 2. Public information before civil awards and scheduled announcements

Before the observation window, select public award calendars, sports scheduling announcements and civil institution releases with independent official sources. Record source publication, local first receipt, version hash, condition mapping, affected outcome and the reviewer's first classification. Distinguish corrections, syndication and leaks already published in other monitored outlets. Predefine the first actionable book change using the existing spread/depth policy. Withhold a chronology where collector downtime covers the alleged lead. Randomly assign placebo event windows and use a human reviewer who cannot see the trade history.

Compare the fraction of alerted and matched non-alert windows preceding every covered relevant source and the first material price change. Stratify by news coverage and price age. Publish the first 100 eligible events or the entire fixed quarter, whichever occurs first. A zero or reversed difference, a date-only timestamp or a missed-source control invalidates the corresponding lead. This tests the value of the review join and does not identify what any account knew. [Implemented join](../../monitoring/radar/context.py)

## 3. Shared routes and matched service controls

Freeze a consecutive set of Arbitrum stablecoin bridge orders and obtain at least one independent archive source. Sample candidate and control intermediary windows before reading downstream betting results. Match on the published route, chain, token contract, week, inbound-size band and intermediary throughput. Require receipt-level amount equality, chronological ordering, complete logs and opening/closing balances. Include known service fan-out, reversed-order and shuffled-week controls. Count distinct intermediaries and events, with uncertainty clustered on the intermediary. Publish missed or censored RPC calls.

Primary endpoint is excess balanced pass-through frequency in the case cohort versus matched public traffic. Continue to 100 independent intermediaries per group or the preregistered eight-week limit. Report effective sample and attainable precision. Common service traffic producing the same pattern refutes its value as a discriminator while leaving the underlying transfers verified. The three original pairs are development examples and must be excluded from the future test. [Existing screen](RELAY_PROTOCOL.md) · [Primary-chain receipts](arbitrum_receipts.json)
