# Alert timing, forecast and replication protocol

Fixed on 21 November 2026 on top of `3faf43ae4b7bf8859afe2ab3c1543aa01169608a`. Published results of earlier work, including the Nobel and the sports bucket, were known before this plan. Existing data make the new statistical tests retrospective. No trading, alert tuning or wallet attribution.

1. **Observation integrity.** The first eligible history point must be at or after the target, at most 600 seconds late and no later than the evaluation clock. This carries the existing query's ten-minute tail to each individual target. Entry and the 1 h, 6 h and 24 h horizons are timed from emission. A missing point stays missing. Retain the actual chosen timestamp, and never call an averaged chart point an executable price. Compare the old and new samplers on all minute marks of the 12 hours before the official 2025 Nobel announcement, on the archived 10-minute Machado Yes chart and independently on its recorded Yes trade tape. At one hour after each simulated emission, count future points selected by the old sampler. At maturity, count withheld late values, missing values and observations beyond archive end separately. Report the same diagnostic across tokens on the committed quote archive, using one simulated emission per UTC hour inside each token's capture. These grid times are measurement probes, not actual alerts. Same-second conflicting prices have no unique order unless the source provides one. Sensitivity at 60 and 300 seconds is descriptive only.
2. **Forecast benchmark.** Take all VALIDATION rows from the committed early-detection analytic table. Group by event. Use UTC 2025-07-01 and 2026-01-01 as split boundaries. Retain only events whose complete set of included signals falls within one split. TRAIN and VALID must also have every market closed before the next split. Purge a decision at or after that market's close. Keep the original universe limitations (resolved, volume-selected markets and ignition windows). On TRAIN fit only the mean of outcome minus the price at the alert in each of three fixed screens: repository LONELY_THIN, concentration alone (LONELY), and a longshot flow screen inspired by ACDC (initiator bought at least USD 2,500 over the archived window and outcome price at the alert at most 0.35). ACDC uses individual bets; this is a distinct adaptation. Predictions outside a screen equal the market price, inside it add the fitted offset, bounded to [0,1]. Map an empty fit to zero and disclose it. Never refit or select on VALID or TEST. Report eligible events, markets, alerts, coverage, mean excess and Brier improvement against the same price on the same rows. Primary measure is the TEST improvement across the whole cohort. Resample entire events with replacement, 5,000 replicates, seed 20261121. Use simultaneous two-sided Bonferroni percentile intervals across the three candidates (98.333% each); a lower bound strictly above zero is necessary for predictive validation, with at least 30 distinct affected TEST events and positive VALID improvement. Report price-band and calendar sensitivity where meaningful. Historical prints are not fillable quotes. Insider precision and trading profitability are separate unmeasured outcomes.
3. **External replication.** Recompute counts from ForesightFlow's FFIC inventory and a published wallet-skill or calibration statistic from pinned third-party data. Read code and execution assumptions before drawing comparisons. Test the published sports T−7d bucket [0.55,0.60) on the separate GitHubMagnus archive if category/price/timing permit, as a transfer diagnostic. Retain only observations available by the target and show game-market eligibility, event counts and source mismatch. Neither dataset's published outcome distribution is blind. No external raw dataset or program is republished.
4. **Public-source pilot.** Select the first quoted, active binary tennis contract returned by the official Gamma tennis event query (if necessary page to first active contract). Freeze its identifier before sampling the official Data API three times, at least 30 s apart. Record request/response clocks, series timestamps, errors, repeated observations and freshness; use a closed window or as-of instant per poll. No substitution of another market after observing the first answer. This measures the read path, not detector recall or a user-facing alert.
5. **Verdict.** A better causal measurement does not itself validate a classifier. An executable edge additionally requires synchronized depth and fees, capacity, delay, a genuinely new prospective cohort and an untouched final test. No archive principal or identified wallet serves as a universal labelled positive.
