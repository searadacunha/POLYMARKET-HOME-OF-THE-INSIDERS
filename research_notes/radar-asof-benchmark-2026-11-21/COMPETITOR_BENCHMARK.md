# Comparison with public Polymarket research

Checked 21 November 2026 against main revision `3faf43a`. We found **43 distinct GitHub projects**, including the 32 in the two preceding repository audits. Sixteen had selected implementation or data files inspected, two served as directories, 24 had only their remote HEAD verified and one requested repository could not be retrieved. These counts describe access and review depth. Stars, titles and a live-looking dashboard earn no evidence credit. The detailed comparison below covers 14 projects. The manifest also includes the hashes of the two further implementation reviews, Vedant-29 and darrnhard. ### Publications, official sources and discovery

Inca Digital reports 39 accounts flagged in a preselected group of 488 profitable traders. That denominator is unsuitable for estimating the fraction of all alerts that warrant investigation. ACDC publishes a large-bet/low-price screen with an individual-bet definition. Mitts and Ofir bring a much wider market universe. Columbia's wash-trading research provides a competing explanation for unusual volume. We did not reconstruct these authors' full samples. [Inca Digital](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/incadigital_insider-trading-on-prediction-markets-a-activity-7480960443440590848-iBIM) · [ACDC](https://acdatacollective.org/work/anti-corruption-data-collective-urges-cftc-to-put-a-stop-to-prediction-market-betting-on-war/) · [Mitts and Ofir](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/03/25/from-iran-to-taylor-swift-informed-trading-in-prediction-markets/) · [Columbia wash-trading paper](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/citation_file_upload/ssrn-5714122.pdf)

Jia and Sun describe order-book-aware paper and live testing. ForesightFlow describes real-time coordination analysis. Both are stronger candidates for direct comparison on a future shared stream than an ex-post wallet ranking. Published conclusions and the public FFIC case index have different evidential status. Studies of trader skill use differing eligibility and execution assumptions. A short split-half null cannot settle the broader literature. [Jia and Sun](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34754) · [ForesightFlow paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00493) · [FFIC inventory page](https://www.foresightflow.org/datasets/ffic) · [Cram et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6617059)

Dune's per-fill catalogue provides a second universe for coverage checks. Its indexing and aggregation rules would need an explicit crosswalk before treating a missing fill as a monitoring failure. Official regulatory material supplies attributable statements. Nobel reporting shows that the unusual betting was already public in October 2025. Neither a press report, a social post, nor a policy document assigns a trader's private information set. GitHub directories, scholarly discovery searches, X evidence already archived in this repository and Reddit served as discovery channels. Unarchived search results supplied no positive labels. [Dune](https://dune.com/markets/prediction-markets/polymarket) · [CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9185-26) · [October 2025 reporting](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/oct/10/nobel-peace-prize-bets-polymarket) · [Reuters follow-up](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/nobel-committee-says-peace-prize-winner-likely-revealed-early-by-digital-spying-2026-01-30/) · [Archived X reconciliation](../x-corpus-reconciliation-2026-11-06/) · [Reddit lead](https://www.reddit.com/r/PolymarketTrading/comments/1q44m6y/psa_why_most_whaleinsider_polymarket_posts_are/)
