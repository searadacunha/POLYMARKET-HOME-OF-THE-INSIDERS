# The Maduro and Iran markets

On-chain investigation of the Maduro (January 3, 2026) and Iran (February 28, 2026) markets: network of accounts, funding, then the search for a detectable and executable signal, phase by phase.

**State:** Phases 1 to 15 written. The final evaluation of phase 11 (100 alerts or 6 weeks) has no report: collection stopped, last `ws_market` folder of 2026-10-09, logs up to 2026-10-16, no `p11` to `p15` process on November 2.

## Reproduce

One folder per phase: `PROTOCOLE_PN.md`, `RAPPORT_PHASEN.md`, scripts in `sources/scripts/` (see `sources/phase15/README_PHASE15.md`). Symbolic links from phases to `scripts/` are not kept: the manifest gives their target.

## Contents

Snapshot of November 2, 2026. `sources/` is a verbatim copy of the original working tree, mostly in French, with relative paths kept. `data_manifest.json` lists every original file, published or not, and why. `local_only_hashes.sha256.gz`, when present, holds the hashes of large unpublished sets.
