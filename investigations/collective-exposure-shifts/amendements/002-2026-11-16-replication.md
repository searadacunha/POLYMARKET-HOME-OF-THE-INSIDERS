# Amendment 002, 2026-11-16, pre-declared replication on an expanded sample

**Status: adopted and written before the expanded sample was collected.**

## Why

The frozen decision rule needs 30 alerts in TEST to say `NOT_SUPPORTED` rather than `INCONCLUSIVE`. The primary
sample delivered 16. A study that stops at 16 cannot separate "no effect" from "no power", and the mission asks for
an answer, not a shrug.

## What is declared, before any expanded data exists

- **Same rule, same code, same thresholds, same seeds, same splits, same decision rule.** Nothing is tuned.
- **Expanded sample:** the same population and the same hash rule with the threshold moved from
  `hash mod 1000 < 25` to `hash mod 1000 < 50`, 1,579 markets instead of 763. The slice for `< 25` is a strict
  subset of the `< 50` slice, so the primary sample is contained in the replication; the two are reported separately
  and the replication is **not** called a confirmation of itself.
- **Role of each run:**
  - primary (763 markets, 129 S4 alerts, 16 in TEST), decides nothing new, reported as is;
  - replication (1,579 markets), supplies the TEST count needed by the declared rule, and is reported as a separate
    table. If the replication TEST result contradicts the primary, that contradiction is the finding.
- **No stopping rule on the headline number.** The replication is run once, in full.

## Known cost

Doubling the sample doubles the multiple-comparison exposure of the sweep cells. They were already declared in the
plan and are counted in the family of 10; the replication does not add new cells.
