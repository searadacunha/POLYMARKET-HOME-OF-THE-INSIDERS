# Amendment 001, 2026-11-17T09:40:58Z, after the independent audit of the v1 replay

**Written after v1 results were seen** (v1 outputs kept unchanged in `out_v1/`, GRID.json unchanged,
sha256 6a99a1a1…5e2c). Nothing here changes the stop rule or the verdict, which the v1 statistics gate fixed.
The changes answer audit findings; they are listed so that no v1/v2 difference is silent.

## Corrections of implementation defects (v2 replaces v1 for these)

1. **Reference price of the causal trigger.** v1 centred the price-only control band, the execution-cost metric
   and FALSE_POSITIVES on the frozen alert's price, observed at the frozen time, which is later than the causal
   trigger for 117 of 266 alerts (a look-ahead tied to the outcome). v2 uses the causal candidate's own price for
   the causal trigger, the frozen price for the frozen trigger.
2. **Price-only control.** v2 counts drawn and filled control trades (v1 dropped unfilled draws silently) and
   excludes grid times within 6 h of ANY S4 candidate of the same (market, side), not only the first.
3. **Capacity.** v2 separates "no transaction in the window" from "transactions present but all ambiguous"
   (NO_TX vs AMBIGUOUS_ONLY vs NO_ASK_EVIDENCE); the median observed ask is reported only on windows with
   identified takers.
4. **Verdict code** now follows GRID.json: a valid signal with non-positive hypothetical PnL gives
   NO_DEMONSTRATED_EDGE, not UNVERIFIABLE. The stats gate fails loudly on an unknown schema instead of reading it
   as "no significant test".
5. **FALSE_POSITIVES.csv** marks non-executable losing alerts as such (empty PnL) instead of 0.
6. **No bytecode** is written into the frozen study (two .pyc files written by v1 at 09:27-09:28 were removed;
   PROTOCOL.lock hashes re-checked: intact).

## Added descriptive readings (no new test, no p-value)

7. **Selection by later activity.** Executed alerts are those followed by a multi-maker sweep of the side, a
   condition after the trigger that may correlate with the outcome. v2 reports, for executable and
   non-executable alerts separately, the win rate, mean price and gross payoff at the trigger price.
8. **Detection gate.** The frozen detector only evaluates a grid time once a later fill exists (t <= last_ts).
   v2 reports the delay from the causal grid time to the first fill at or after it (the earliest moment the frozen
   code, run live, could emit the alert).

## Paper trader fixes (before any prospective record is used)

9. A first S4 candidate is live if its grid time is at or after the previous cycle's newest grid point
   (stored in state), not only the current one; real detection time = after the market's tape top-up.
10. Tape top-up replaces the fetched time range instead of de-duplicating rows (identical maker fills inside one
    transaction are legitimate and were lost by the unique constraint).
11. Network errors on the book snapshot are logged and do not stop the cycle; the journal hash chain keeps the
    last hash in a side file and refuses to append after a partial line.

Unchanged: population, alerts, latency, size and cost grids, fill model, headline cell, stop rule.
