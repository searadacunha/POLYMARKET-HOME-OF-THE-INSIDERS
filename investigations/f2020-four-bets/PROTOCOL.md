# Four prespecified first buys after mixed-route receipts
Frozen on 22 November 2026 before reading the executions, settlements or subsequent outcomes in this follow-up. The subjects and their funding-to-first-buy times were known from PR 110. This is a descriptive, selected case series.

Anchor each case to its supplied Polygon transaction and the unique exact trading recipient in the source ledger. Retain discrepancies with supplied addresses. Verify each token using the market token map and the chain transfer. Use the contract payout vector and market rules for resolution. Separate individual execution cash flow, the complete wallet/outcome-token position, other outcomes of the same market and subsequent on-platform trading. Unsettled inventory, transfers, previous inventory, unresolved fees, batch attribution and missing history have separate states. A notional is not a wallet debit. A winning outcome alone is not proof of profit. Round only for display.

Follow activity to the latest fixed UTC snapshot cutoff. Include buys, sells, redemptions, splits, merges and transfers where observable. Keep losing and open cases. Compare the same wallet's trading during an equal-length pre-funding period where available. Controls are other observed buyers on the same market as the index execution. Select without looking at outcomes. Compare timing, size and buy direction or entry-price bands. A near-resolution trade is descriptive unless its ordering relative to a demonstrably new public observation is established. For Bitcoin compare with the start/end oracle window. For Busan use the station and rule recorded by the market.

For ranking, prioritise repeated positive risk-adjusted surprises with known pre-trade prices, then unusual profitable single-market concentration and temporal proximity to a verified information release, then weaker testable leads. Label descriptive, selected controls and limited power explicitly. Separate opacity of the financial rail, trading anomaly and apparent informational advantage. No inference of ownership or unlawful conduct. No causal post-funding PnL is assumed.
