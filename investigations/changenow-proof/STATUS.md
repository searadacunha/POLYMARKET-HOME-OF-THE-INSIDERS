## Objective

Establish, address by address, which accounts really touched ChangeNOW, for what amounts, and how far a single order can be found with public data.

## Actual state

Final report written on November 2. Nothing was sent to ChangeNOW or any third party, no transaction was issued. An address is not a person: no identity is established or sought here.

## Conclusions

One account only, kraben006, has a corroborated direct contact, with no documented path to its bets. On the scale of the reports, its two received payments and the sweep into the service reach level 2 of 4. The hop from its key to the relay stays at level 0, because the relay carries no label. No individual order found, so neither level 3 nor level 4. Monero: 0 confirmed case.

## Limits

Attribution without confirmation from ChangeNOW. Partial windows: never the full history of the service wallets. BSC, Base, Solana and Tron barely read or not at all. A conclusions audit by an agent of the same model family returned FAIL on levels and wording: its points were checked against the evidence and fixed, without a second audit and without human review.
