# Frozen analysis plan · 22 November 2026 UTC

Freeze before joining new recipient logs to Polymarket activity. The first reporting window is the last eight **closed** ISO weeks on the freeze date, W39–W46. The week of the final credit uses Monday 00:00:00 UTC inclusive and the next Monday exclusive. Extend the historical replay to the first dated observation that the adapters actually ingest. Preserve gaps. Historical data are retrospective even if an analyst has not seen the join.

Count a trading address on Polygon once per service/week. Identify it by execution as the exact proxy/account. A payment to a signer and a derived proxy are separate addresses. Count direct receipts from explicitly tagged hot wallets or decoded official pool withdrawals. Track the confidence in the provider label separately. Classify transfers from the commingled f2020 route as POSSIBLE even when paid directly to a trader. Deduplicate using (chain ID, transaction hash, log index). Use the final receipt once for volume, retain all legs, stop at custody or mixed balances. Only count an intermediate token hop with chronological, same-asset, independently checked transfers and a positive conservative attributable amount. Treat unmatched funding as unknown. Tornado exposure starts at the withdrawal event and never identifies the depositor.

## Statistical endpoints and blinding

Unit: distinct trading account, first eligible verified funding within an ISO week. Keep only the first observation per account in a test partition. Eligible controls are drawn independently of funding or future outcomes, on the same chain and ISO week, with an independently dated funding event, equal exposure ascertainment and a complete observation window. No case recruited through the payment address itself can serve as its own control. Exclude users of either tracked service from controls. Match without replacement by exact funding week and market liquidity quintile and within a factor of two in prior 30-day executed volume, account age in days and new funding size. Use only information available before the funding. Rank matches by summed absolute log distances, break ties lexicographically. Disclose control attrition.

Test five two-sided endpoints separately per service (ten comparisons, Holm familywise alpha .05). (1) Difference in 7-day buy-notional Herfindahl by market. (2) Difference in share of buys before a midprice move of at least 10 percentage points towards the purchased outcome within 6 hours. (3) Difference in share of the account's net buy exposure changes accompanied by another distinct cohort member buying the same outcome in a 30-minute bin. (4) Difference in 30-day marked net return after fees, with executable marks and all cash flows, including unresolved inventory. (5) Out-of-time Brier-loss improvement over BOTH pre-trade market price and a frozen price-plus-large-bet baseline (large = at least USD 10,000); positive improvement is the smaller of the two paired improvements. Require valid outcome timestamps, causal quote ordering and strictly later held-out dates. No substitution of a retrospective winner list or of service contact for inside information.

The first eight weeks are descriptive. Freeze weights on historical data through W43, quarantine W44 (and purge training outcomes overlapping a test feature window); W45 onward is temporal holdout. Require each case's outcome horizon to have elapsed, at least 20 disjoint eligible pairs per endpoint/service, and complete field-specific observability. Inference: paired sign-flip permutation at the independent pair level after excluding shared-market/overlapping-window dependencies, 19,999 draws (seed 20261122), Holm across all ten including untested entries as p=1. If inputs, power gate or independence are unavailable report NOT_TESTABLE with the missing inputs. This design estimates conditional associations in the covered cohort; a causal or legal allegation requires independent evidence.
