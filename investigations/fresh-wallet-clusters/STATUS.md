## Objective

Test whether fresh wallets that each commit at least 1,000 USD to the same outcome share a private funder, a private cash-out, or co-timing beyond a matched control.

## Actual state

The pre-registered tests were run on a systematic sample. The test and the validation replication both observed zero private-funder groups and zero private cash-out groups. The only verified link is the already published Nobel 2025 case, recovered as a pipeline check and held out of the rate.

## Conclusions

The central answer on this sample is no. Shared services and co-timed fresh wallets without a private counterparty are recorded as negative results, not as common ownership. Amendment 002 does not change that answer. Amendment 003 raises coverage to 260 complete information-market tapes, 1.0 percent of the 25,789-market catalogue, refutes the 17 direct-handoff pairs, and does not find a new information-market cluster. The parent verdict is not a census. Amendment 004 requests all 25,789 information markets at a 100 USD floor. 24,822 (96.25 percent) are complete within three pages and 967 are partial. H6 on the 29 headline wallets is NOT_OBSERVED. No new information-market cluster was confirmed. Amendment 005 completes 806 of those 967 markets within 15 pages, leaving 161 partial, and reconstructs the Maduro NO position as a cash loss of 59,035.93 USD. H7 is NOT_OBSERVED.

## Limits

The pre-registered sample is not every market. Amendment 004 does request every information market, at a 100 USD floor and at most three pages. Amendment 005 extends the partials to 15 pages. 161 markets still have more fills of at least 100 USD. Fills under 100 USD were not re-read for the 44,752 near-threshold wallet-outcomes. The Maduro position was reconstructed from its 51 fills. Wallet-level sells, redemptions and buys were summed, and open inventory was left unmarked. No new path off Polygon was verified. The Safe signing key on the funding path was not recovered.
