## Objective

Test whether fresh wallets that each commit at least 1,000 USD to the same outcome share a private funder, a private cash-out, or co-timing beyond a matched control.

## Actual state

The pre-registered tests were run on a systematic sample. The test and the validation replication both observed zero private-funder groups and zero private cash-out groups. The only verified link is the already published Nobel 2025 case, recovered as a pipeline check and held out of the rate.

## Conclusions

The central answer on this sample is no. Shared services and co-timed fresh wallets without a private counterparty are recorded as negative results, not as common ownership.

## Limits

The sample is not every market. Fills under 100 USD that never print a larger fill are absent. Funding outside a 10,000-block window, on another chain, or past a contract is not followed. Open markets are described and are not in the three tests. No announcement clock was built.
