## Objective

Test whether fresh wallets that each commit at least 1,000 USD to the same outcome share a private funder, a private cash-out, or co-timing beyond a matched control.

## Actual state

The pre-registered tests were run on a systematic sample. The test and the validation replication both observed zero private-funder groups and zero private cash-out groups. The only verified link is the already published Nobel 2025 case, recovered as a pipeline check and held out of the rate.

## Conclusions

The central answer on this sample is no. Shared services and co-timed fresh wallets without a private counterparty are recorded as negative results, not as common ownership. Amendment 002 does not change that answer. Amendment 003 raises coverage to 260 complete information-market tapes, 1.0 percent of the 25,789-market catalogue, refutes the 17 direct-handoff pairs, and does not find a new information-market cluster. The parent verdict is not a census. Amendment 004 requests all 25,789 information markets at a 100 USD floor. 24,822 (96.25 percent) are complete within three pages and 967 are partial. H6 on the 29 headline wallets is NOT_OBSERVED. No new information-market cluster was confirmed. Amendment 005 completes 806 of those 967 markets within 15 pages, leaving 161 partial, and reconstructs the Maduro NO position as a cash loss of 59,035.93 USD. H7 is NOT_OBSERVED. Amendment 006 names five different Safe owners and completes 134 further markets within 40 pages. 27 markets remain partial. The no-floor re-run of H1, H2 and H3 was not executed.

## Limits

The pre-registered sample is not every market. Amendment 006 leaves 27 markets above 40 pages at a 100 USD floor. Fills under 100 USD were not re-read, and H1, H2 and H3 were not re-run on a no-floor set. The CC-BY archive ends on 24 April 2026. One KPMG Solana signature was re-read. The other published rows were not. Safe owners were read at the chain tip. AddedOwner history was not.
