# Negative results

These are results for the tapes this run read. They are not a claim about markets the sample skipped, about fills under $100 that never printed a larger fill, or about money that arrived outside the 10,000-block window.

- **H1, test × information: NOT_OBSERVED.** Zero market-outcomes had two qualifying fresh wallets inside seven days with the same primary private funder. The shuffle null is also zero. p = 1.0. Eighty markets, fifteen fresh groups, all fifteen traced.
- **H1, validation × information:** the same zero. Seventy-nine markets with a complete tape. This replication was not used to change a threshold and was not promoted to replace the test.
- **H1 on the public-process test markets:** zero. There is no specificity contrast to interpret, because the information side is also zero.
- **H3, private cash-out:** zero in the test and in validation. No plain destination of at least $100 is shared by two traced wallets anywhere in the edge table.
- **H2, 24-hour co-timing:** information markets minus public-process markets is +0.10, p = 0.25, against a corrected threshold of 0.0167. Not a hit. The gap is in the top volume fifth, where the control cell has three markets.
- **Services.** Ten of the fifteen test information groups, eight of the eleven validation information groups, and seven validation control groups share only a service. A shared exchange, bridge, relayer, pool or other contract is not counted as a person.
- **Behavior without a counterparty.** Five test information groups and three validation information groups crossed $1,000 on the same outcome inside seven days and had no shared counterparty in the window. That is common enough, on this sample, that it is not evidence of a joint plan.
- **The Nobel group** is the only `VERIFIED FINANCIAL LINK`. It was a pipeline check against a case this repository already published, and it sat outside the confirmatory denominator. Recovering it shows the tracer can see a plain key. It is not a rate.
- **Failed activity reads were not treated as fresh.** In the confirmatory denominators every eligible wallet had an activity read. The 4,000-wallet cap left 316 of 832 eligible wallets on open markets unread. Open markets are not in the three tests.
- **No announcement clock was built.** Lead time against a headline is not a result of this run.
- **Small plain-key payments and passthrough chains** described in FINDINGS.md were inspected and do not meet the frozen definition of a private link. They are not a quiet positive result.

`INCONCLUSIVE_COVERAGE` was the verdict of an earlier analyze, before the seven-day group members had all been traced (5 of 15). After those traces, the coverage floor is met and the verdict is `NOT_OBSERVED`. The criteria were not changed between the two runs.
